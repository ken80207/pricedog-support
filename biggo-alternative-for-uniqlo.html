BigGo alternatives when you only shop UNIQLO and GU

Last checked 2026-11-27

BigGo fits broad price comparison; PriceDog fits shoppers who only want UNIQLO and GU prices tracked.

Broad versus narrow

A general comparison app covers many categories. A focused one can use UNIQLO and GU specifics such as the 6-digit tag code and Japan prices.

Which one you need depends on how many stores you shop at.

BigGo - 比價比個夠

Better for comparing prices across many online stores and product categories

Where PriceDog fits you want tracking focused on UNIQLO and GU tag codes, colors and sizes, and Taiwan versus Japan prices

GU Taiwan

Better for buying from GU Taiwan's official online store and using the GU member account

Where PriceDog fits you want price history and sale notifications for GU alongside UNIQLO
