How to stop missing UNIQLO sales in Taiwan

Last checked 2026-11-27

Follow the specific items you want instead of checking the official site every day, and turn on push notifications. PriceDog notifies you once a followed UNIQLO or GU item in Taiwan goes on sale.

Why sales get missed

Fast-fashion prices change often, and the only way to catch a drop by hand is to open the site or app again and again. Most people stop checking after a few days.

Set up a sale alert

You need the 6-digit code printed on the product tag, or the product page.

  1. Open PriceDog and type the 6-digit tag code, or scan the barcode.
  2. Tap to track the item. Tracking needs a sign-in with Google or Apple; browsing works as a guest.
  3. Allow notifications when asked.
  4. Wait for the on-sale push.

What the notification does and does not do

It tells you a sale has started. It does not predict when the next Taiwan sale will begin. Notifications are combined into one push per day that lists how many followed items are on sale.

Checking the official site daily is a general approach that works but takes effort. Following items and receiving pushes is what PriceDog automates; it covers UNIQLO and GU only.

FAQ

Does it cover stores other than UNIQLO and GU?

No. PriceDog is built only for UNIQLO and GU.

Do I need an account?

Browsing works as a guest. Tracking a product requires signing in with Google or Apple.

Does it predict the next sale?

No. It notifies you once a sale has started.
