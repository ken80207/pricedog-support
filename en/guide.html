How to use PriceDog

A step-by-step guide to finding UNIQLO and GU items, tracking the styles you want, getting sale notifications, reading the price history and comparing with Japan prices.

Find an item

  1. Type the 6-digit code printed on the product tag into the search bar.
  2. Or open Scan and point the camera at the tag barcode. Use the Taiwan / Auto / Japan switch to match the tag you are scanning.
  3. The camera is used only for barcode scanning. If you prefer not to allow it, type the 6-digit code instead.
  4. Browsing, searching and scanning work as a guest, without an account.

Track the colors and sizes you want

  1. Tracking needs an account: sign in with Google or Apple. Apple sign-in is iPhone only.
  2. On the product page, tap the heart button.
  3. In "Which styles to track?", choose the colors and sizes you want: all of them, several, or just one. Then tap Subscribe.
  4. Adding an item uses 1 tracking slot. Changing the styles later does not use a slot, and you can stop tracking from the same sheet.
  5. Your tracking list and the product page show which styles you chose.

Turn on sale notifications

  1. When PriceDog asks to enable notifications, tap Enable.
  2. If you tapped Later, allow notifications for PriceDog in your phone settings.
  3. You are notified only when a style you picked goes on sale. Notifications are combined into one push per day listing how many followed items are on sale.
  4. PriceDog notifies you once a sale has started. It does not predict when the next sale will start.

Read the price history and labels

  1. The Price History chart on the product page shows the price trend in NT$. Swipe left or right to see different time periods.
  2. Historical Low shows the lowest price recorded for the item, so you can tell a real low from the usual price.
  3. NEW SALE means the item has just gone on sale. LOWEST PRICE means it is at its historical low. Officially Unavailable means it is no longer on the official site.
  4. Prices update daily and are for reference only. Actual prices follow the official brand channels.

Compare with the Japan price

  1. On the product page, the JAPAN row shows the official Japan price in yen, an approximate NT$ amount, and how much cheaper or dearer it is than Taiwan.
  2. When the item is on sale in Japan, the product page also shows the Japan sale period.
  3. Open Rankings and choose Cheaper in Japan to see UNIQLO and GU items that cost less in Japan. Sort by Most saved $ or Top discount %, and filter by All, UNIQLO or GU.
  4. Japan prices include 10% consumption tax. The NT$ amount uses a daily exchange rate and your card rate applies; tax-free shopping and coupons are not included.

Record a haul in Japan

  1. Open Haul and tap Scan your first batch.
  2. Scan the tags of what you buy at UNIQLO or GU in Japan. Each item is added to the batch.
  3. If Taiwan has several versions of the item, pick the one you bought.
  4. The batch shows the Japan total, the Taiwan total and how much you saved. Tap a row's price to correct it to what you actually paid, for example a combo price.
  5. Tap Share to turn your savings into an image you can post on social media.
  6. Your haul is stored on your device.

Filter and sort your tracking list

  1. Open the Interest tab to see the items you track.
  2. Filter by All, On Sale, Latest Deal, Lowest Price or Discontinued.
  3. Sort by Subscription Time, Price or Discount.

Free and Premium

PriceDog is free to download and use: search, scan, price history, on-sale notifications, Japan prices, the ranking and the haul are all included. The free version shows ads, and you watch an ad every 3 products you add to restore your tracking quota.

PriceDog Premium removes ads everywhere in the app and lets you add products without watching ads. Sign in before subscribing. There are monthly (NT$30) and yearly (NT$300) auto-renewing plans on the App Store, with no free trial.

Premium auto-renews unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. Subscribed before? Sign in, then tap Restore Purchases on the subscription page.
