FAQ

How do I start tracking an item?

Type the 6-digit code on the tag or scan the barcode, then add the item to your tracking list. Tracking requires signing in with Google or Apple.

When will I get a notification?

When items you follow go on sale. Notifications are combined into one push per day.

Does it tell me when the next sale starts?

No. PriceDog notifies you once a sale has started; it does not predict sale dates. For Japan, the product page shows the current Japan sale period.

Can I track only one color or size?

Yes. Choose the colors and sizes you want (several or all), and you are notified only when one of them goes on sale.

Which brands are supported?

UNIQLO and GU only.

Can I use it in Japan?

Yes. Scan tags at UNIQLO or GU stores in Japan to see the Taiwan price and the difference. Japan prices include 10% consumption tax and the NT$ amount uses a daily exchange rate; tax-free shopping and coupons are not included.

Is it free?

Yes, with ads. On the free version you watch an ad every 3 products you add. PriceDog Premium is a auto-renewing subscription at NT$30 per month or NT$300 per year with no ads. There is no free trial.

Why does it need the camera?

Only for barcode scanning. You can type the 6-digit code instead.

Where can I download it?

On the Taiwan App Store (iOS 17.0 or later) and on Google Play.
