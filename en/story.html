Why PriceDog exists

UNIQLO and GU prices in Taiwan change often, and it is easy to miss a sale. The usual answer is checking the official site every day, and even when a price drops, it is hard to tell whether it is a real low or the price the item usually sells for.

PriceDog does one job: tell you when an item you want goes on sale, and whether the current price is a good one. It covers only UNIQLO and GU, works with the 6-digit code on the tag and the in-store barcode, and tracks the colors and sizes you pick rather than just the product.

It also answers a question for Taiwanese shoppers in Japan: is this cheaper than at home? The product page shows the Japan price and sale period next to the Taiwan price, and the Haul totals how much a trip saved against Taiwan prices.

What it does not do: it is not a general price comparison app, and it does not predict when the next sale will start; it notifies you once a sale has begun. Prices update daily and are for reference only, and actual prices follow the official brand channels. PriceDog is an independent app, not an official UNIQLO or GU app.

PriceDog is made by Kennily (Ken), an independent developer who also makes Deerly. Questions and ideas go straight to the developer at kennily251107@gmail.com.
