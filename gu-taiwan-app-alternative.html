GU Taiwan app alternatives for tracking GU prices

Last checked 2026-11-27

If you want to buy from GU Taiwan and use your member account, the official GU Taiwan app is the place. If you want to know when a GU item you are watching goes on sale, and whether that price is a good one, a price tracker such as PriceDog does a different job.

What the official GU Taiwan app is for

The GU Taiwan app is GU's own store: you browse, buy from the online store and use your GU member account and its offers. Its App Store listing describes member coupons, online-only sizes, favorites, restock notifications for some sold-out items and news push notifications.

What a price tracker adds

A tracker watches the price over time. In PriceDog you enter the 6-digit code on the tag or scan the barcode, pick the colors and sizes you care about, and get a push notification when a style you picked goes on sale. The price history chart in NT$ helps you judge whether today's price is a real low.

Using both

  1. Find the item you want in a GU store or online.
  2. Track it in PriceDog with the tag code or barcode and pick your colors and sizes.
  3. When PriceDog notifies you of a sale, check the price history, then buy wherever you prefer, including the GU Taiwan app.

Any shopper can wait for a sale by checking the official app every day. PriceDog only tracks UNIQLO and GU prices; it does not sell clothes, and you still buy from GU.

FAQ

Does PriceDog replace the GU Taiwan app?

No. PriceDog tracks prices and notifies you of sales; buying and member offers stay in GU's own app or store.

Does PriceDog cover GU as well as UNIQLO?

Yes. PriceDog tracks both UNIQLO and GU Taiwan prices.
