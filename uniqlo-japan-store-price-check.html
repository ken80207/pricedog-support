In a UNIQLO store in Japan: cheaper than Taiwan?

Last checked 2026-11-27

Scan the tag barcode with PriceDog's region set to Japan or Auto. The product page shows the official Japan price in yen with an approximate NT$ amount and whether it is cheaper or dearer than Taiwan.

Check in the store

  1. Open the scan screen and choose Taiwan, Auto or Japan as the region.
  2. Scan the barcode on the tag.
  3. Read the Japan price row on the product page.

Before you rely on the number

Japan prices include 10% consumption tax. The NT$ amount uses a daily exchange rate, and your card rate will differ. Tax-free shopping and coupons are not included.

Browse in advance

A ranking lists UNIQLO and GU items that are cheaper in Japan than in Taiwan, sortable by amount saved or discount percentage and filterable by brand.

Converting yen by hand is the general method. The Japan price row, sale period and ranking are app features.

FAQ

Does it show the Japan sale period?

Yes, when the item is on sale in Japan.

Are tax-free prices included?

No.
