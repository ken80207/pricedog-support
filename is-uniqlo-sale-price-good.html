How to tell if a UNIQLO sale price is a good deal

Last checked 2026-11-27

Compare the sale price with the item's price history. If PriceDog labels it as a historical low, it is at the lowest price recorded for that item; if the chart shows it often sells at that price, it is the usual price.

What to check

A sale tag alone does not say whether the price is unusual. The history does.

  1. Open the product page in PriceDog.
  2. Read the price history chart in NT$.
  3. Look for the historical low label or the newly on sale label.
  4. If you are in Japan, check the Japan price too.

Limits

Prices update daily and are for reference only. PriceDog cannot predict whether the price will fall further.

Comparing with last year's memory is a general approach. The chart and labels are app features.

FAQ

Can it tell me when the next sale is?

No, it notifies you once a sale has started.

Check the history before you buy.
