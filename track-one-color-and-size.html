Only want one color and size? Track just that style

Last checked 2026-11-27

When you track an item in PriceDog, choose only the colors and sizes you want. You are notified only when a style you picked goes on sale.

Pick your styles

  1. Open the product page and start tracking.
  2. In the color and size sheet, choose several or all.
  3. Confirm. The tracking list and product page show the styles you chose.

Why it helps

A product can have many colors and sizes, and not all go on sale together. Tracking your style avoids pushes you do not care about.

Watching one size by hand is the general approach. Style-level tracking is an app feature.

FAQ

Can I pick several colors?

Yes, several or all.

Pick your style and track it.
